# Buying Plan, October 2026 to January 2027

**Draft, 2026-10-04.** Every month stays at or near the 150 EUR hobby cap. Pre-orders count in the month you order them. Walt's shipping is about 9 per order under 150, and Walt's holds an order until everything in it is released, so in-stock books and far pre-orders need separate orders. The numbers depend on the real payday leftover (cap = leftover minus the savings floor), real Walt's stock, and prices I have not seen yet (marked *est*).
