# Thor

Rough start (constant "Father help", 60-second rule); improved from Epic 3. Read Epic 1-4; liked 3-4 more. Epic 4 devoured in a day (Mangog was a standout).

## Why I read it
I love mythologies. I didn't like him always asking his father for help, and I still don't like the 60-second rule (let go of the hammer for a minute and he turns back into Don Blake), but it's better now.

## Plan and status

- Read up to Epic 4. Next: Epic 5 (in Omnibus 3). Then Omnibus 4 (85, last copy; fallback Epic 6 at Cheap-Comics) and Omnibus 5 (104.99).
- Later: Simonson omnibus (Epic 13 and the unreleased 14-15 stretch).
- Context: Thor was killed in *Immortal Thor* #24 (2025); the modern Thor titles continue with new characters. Modern: Thor Gorr Premier (Mar 9).
