# Spider-Man

Read Epic 1-2. Early Spidey feels rude/aggressive compared to the modern version.

## Why I read it
Spidey is Spidey: I grew up with lots of Spider-Man movies, cartoons and games. I've only read 2 Epics, so my take on the early years is that he's a lot ruder and meaner than I'm used to.
