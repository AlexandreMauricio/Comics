## Iron Man Epic 16 (War Games, #258-277) workaround
- **Iron Man: Armor Wars Omnibus** (768 pages, Mark Bright cover): Iron Man #219-232, #258-266, Iron Man & the Armor Wars #1-4 (2009), Iron Man #258.1-258.4. Released Jan 2026 (RRP 100 EUR). Walt's listed it at 78.99 (21% off), but the page still said "pre-order", so check real stock.
- **Iron Man: The Dragon Seed Saga TPB** (2008, ISBN 9780785131311): Iron Man #270-275.
- Together they cover #258-266 and #270-275, which is 15 of 20 issues of Epic 16.
- **Still missing: #267-269 and #276-277** (5 issues). Options: singles, or later find Epic 16 at a fair price.
- The omnibus also repeats #219-232 (already in owned Epic 13) and adds the Armor Wars II and 2009 material.
- Release date: sources say Jan 13, 2026 (already out), but Walt's still shows it as a pre-order. If the real date turns out to be Jan 2027, it fits the Q1 quarterly pre-order placed in December. Check the Walt's page date.
- Cost: about 79 + 30 = ~109. Not urgent for reading (Epic 16 is many books away), but omnibuses can sell out. Candidate for the November/December monthly cart or a quarterly slot.

## Iron Man Epic 9 stretch
- Iron Man by Michelinie, Layton and Romita Jr Omnibus: Iron Man #115-157. Marvel reprint announced for April 2027. Covers Demon in a Bottle, Doom in Camelot, Hammer, stealth and space armors. Repeats Epic 8 (#115-139) and covers the Epic 9 gap (#140-157).

## Modern Doctor Strange (palate cleanser while Strange is blocked)
- Jason Aaron, *Doctor Strange* vol 4 #1-26 (2015-2017), art Chris Bachalo. Consistently described as the modern entry point. Includes "The Last Days of Magic".
- Mark Waid run (2018-2019), Strange as a "reality surgeon", science meets mysticism. Loki as Sorcerer Supreme is part of this stretch (details unverified).
- A Donny Cates Strange run appeared in one search result (unverified, I'd check before treating it as a real run).
- Suggestion: Aaron is the natural first pick. Do this only after Epic 5 if the gap really bites.

## Events on the radar
- **Secret Wars (1984-85):** 12-issue series. Marvel Super Heroes Secret Wars Omnibus, 2nd edition (Nov 2022), 496 pages, collects #1-12 plus Thor #383, She-Hulk #10, What If #4 and #114. Also a facsimile Archive Edition (Sep 2025, 512 pages). Best fit: read it alongside Iron Man Epic 11 (#178-195, 1983-85), which overlaps its publication dates. Spider-Man's black costume aftermath is ASM #252-262.
- **King in Black:** sequel to Absolute Carnage (Donny Cates, Stegman). Options: King in Black Omnibus (2026 edition, includes all tie-ins), *Venom by Donny Cates Vol. 6: King in Black* (Venom #31-35), or the Venomnibus (whole Cates Venom saga including Absolute Carnage). To read the main story only: King in Black #1-5 plus Venom #31-35. Since you already read Absolute Carnage, the cheapest path is the Vol. 6 TPB, with the omnibus as the completist route. How this connects to your December Venom War omnibus: I did not verify it.
