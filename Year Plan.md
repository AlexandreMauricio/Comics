# Year Plan, October 2026 to September 2027

**Draft v7.1, 2026-11-16. No birthday cart, no extra purchases now, debt coming down.** Replaces v7 (which wrongly bought Thor Omnibus 4 this month). Rules:
1. **Reading order first.** Every line keeps 1 or 2 books to read next and 1 or 2 ahead, bought in order. A book is never bought before the books before it.
2. **Nothing is removed and no route is skipped**, but **timing moves**: when money is short a book buys later, never earlier than its turn needs.
3. **Iron Man is line #1**, Strange #2, Thor #3 in priority; all lines read at the same pace when money allows ([[Reading Rotation]]).
4. **Orders are grouped:** Walt's charges 12 under 150 and ships free from 150 (confirmed); Cheap-Comics is a flat 20 and needs three books; Amazon.es is free with Prime. Pre-orders that release within about two months of each other go in one order.
5. **Allowance: 150 a month, all-in; overspend is taken off the next month** (about 123 from July, to confirm). There is **no birthday cart this year** (big expenses), so everything is paid from the allowance.
6. **This version spends less than the allowance every month from December on, so the debt falls.** About 20 a month of the allowance is kept free for follow-ups (*Venom* #2 and the like); those are not in the table.
