# Side Reads

Add-ons found on comicbookreadingorders.com (2026-11-06), plus what I checked. These are **not** in the Epic order: they are retellings and gap-fillers. They never replace the Epic Collections, which stay the main path because you want every story. They are bought only after the line books (see [[Year Plan]]), and the cheap ones can go inside the December birthday money, since that is outside the 150. Prices are what you saw on Vinted; shipping is usually about 4 each.

## What the site skips
The site's "start here" order jumps ahead (Iron Man from a few Tales of Suspense straight to #113, Daredevil to #158). You want every story, so the Epic order stays. The add-ons above fill nothing that the Epics lack. They only give a second telling of the same ground.

## Where these could go
- **Cheap, inside the December birthday money (about 47 for the three):** *Iron Age* (15.35), *Man Without Fear* (11.15), *Son of Asgard* (8.58), each plus about 4 shipping. The cart then has about 25 free.
- **Later, when the debt is gone (June):** *Enter the Mandarin*, the Spider-Man books, the Untold Tales omnibus.
