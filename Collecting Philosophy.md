# Collecting Philosophy

## Formats
- Physical only. Classic Marvel: **Epic Collections** preferred, **Omnibuses** as substitutes when Epics are unavailable. Modern: TPBs. Singles only as trophies/covers, convention finds, upgrades, or "bridge" singles before a TPB.
- **The roadmap is Epic-numbered.** Omnis are physical substitutes; they never replace Epic numbers (e.g. DD Omni 2 does not remove DD Epic 3/4 from the roadmap).
- Read in chronological order. Don't buy or read later issues of a run while earlier ones are unread. No digital (tried Marvel Unlimited).
- 1-2 books active at a time, no rigid schedule.

## Two kinds of priority (don't confuse them)
- **Reading priority:** what to read next.
- **Availability priority:** what to buy now because it may go OOP or get expensive.

A book I own but can't reach (blocked) doesn't count as next-reading.

## Modern material
TPB is the default; singles only to test a new run, as a cover trophy, or as a bridge before a TPB. Full policy and the current runs are in [[Modern Runs]].

## Priorities (confirmed 2026-11-06)
- **Line books first, bought in reading order.** Never buy a book before the books that come before it in the line. Every line keeps 1 or 2 books to read next and 1 or 2 ahead.
- **Iron Man is line #1.** Strange is #2. Modern books are palate cleansers and matter little; they only ride along when they cost nothing extra.
- **Nothing is dropped.** The plan may run over the allowance and be paid back later (decided 2026-11-07); only timing moves.
- **Securing a book matters more than when the money leaves.** Paying early for a last copy, an out-of-print book or a pre-order price is fine.
- **Group orders to save shipping.** Walt's charges 12 under 150 (it was 9 until Oct 2026) and ships free from 150, Cheap-Comics is a flat 20 and needs three books, Amazon.es is free with Prime. Pre-orders that release within about two months of each other go in one order.
- **New modern runs:** trial singles (#1, #2) only on the FOC pre-order, otherwise wait for the first TPB. **Exception (2026-11-09):** if you liked a book, its follow-up (#2, then the TPB) can be bought as it comes. About 20 a month of the 150 is kept free for these follow-ups.

## Budget
- **Monthly hobby allowance: 150, all-in** (comics, games, subscriptions, every other hobby). A budget month runs from the 24th to the 23rd and is named after the cart placed on that 24th (Sep 24 - Oct 23 is the September budget). Payday moves between the 23rd and the 26th.
  - **Overshoot rule (confirmed 2026-10-10):** whatever a month spends over 150 is **taken off the next month's allowance**; it can push several months to 0. The running balance is kept in [[Year Plan]].
  - **From July 2027 the allowance is expected to be about 123** (to confirm).
  - The personal figures behind the allowance (income, bills, savings) are not kept in this vault.
  - **Birthday and Christmas cart:** up to 300, outside the 150 (December).
  - **Step-up rule:** the allowance may rise to 200 only after a couple of months at or under 150 AND the user confirms the personal big-items pot is on track.
  - **Reading supply rule:** always at least 1 book to read every month, preferably 2 (he reads fast).
- **Pre-orders count inside the allowance and Walt's charges them at order time.** Each has an FOC (final order cutoff) date; ordering before it gives the pre-order price (for example the Q4 Epics were 39.50 instead of 44.99). Record `foc` and `preorder_price` on the note.
- Walt's holds an order until every item in it is released and won't take pre-orders in one order whose releases are more than about 2 months apart. Never mix a far pre-order into an order of in-stock books you want now.
- Roll-over of unspent allowance: **undecided**. Ask before relying on it.
- Don't buy filler for free shipping, except a modern book you want anyway.
- Don't buy mediocre singles just because they're singles.
