# Modern Runs

Research from 2026-09-29 (Marvel, retailer and Wikipedia pages found by web search). Dates for future issues are Marvel's current schedule and can move.

## Policy for modern material
1. **Default format is the TPB**, pre-ordered at Walt's when it's listed. It is 2 to 2.5 times cheaper per issue than singles (about 17-20 EUR for 6 issues vs 6-8 EUR per issue, so 36-48 EUR).
2. **Singles are for three things only:**
   - **Testing a new run** before any TPB exists: buy #1 (maybe #2), then the TPB when it appears.
   - **Trophies:** a cover you really want (like the Skottie Young connecting covers).
   - **Bridge:** a TPB is 5-6 months away, you're hooked, and you'd rather read now. You pay about 15-20 EUR extra per TPB to read 5-6 months earlier.
3. **Don't buy singles and the TPB that contains them in the same cart** unless the singles are a deliberate trophy.
4. Chronology still rules: don't buy #12/#13 while #7-11 are unread.
5. Older singles get scarce and pricey (Iron Man 2026 #2 is 25 EUR for a cover you dislike), so waiting for a TPB risks the singles, not the story.

## Iron Man (2026), Joshua Williamson and Carmen Carnero
- Started **Jan 28, 2026**. New Model 75 armor. Madame Masque and A.I.M. set out to make "the next Tony Stark"; Whiplash and a new Citizen V appear in the later issues.
- Issues so far, roughly monthly: #1 Jan 28, #2 Feb 25, #7 Jul 1, #9 Sep 9. **#10 comes out Oct 14, #12 on Dec 2, 2026.**
- **Vol 1 "A New Nightmare"** (#1-6): Oct 27, 2026, 9781302969066. About 4 months after #6.
- **Vol 2** (#7-11): May 4, 2027, about 5-6 months after #11. Vol 3 is not announced. If the gap repeats, it would be around the fall of 2027 (my inference, not announced).
- **Skottie Young 4-part connecting variant** covers exist for **#1 to #4 only** (I found nothing for #5-6), so the Vinted set of four is the complete thing.
- What Vol 1 costs you: TPB 17 (20 undiscounted). Buying #1-6 as singles would be 36-48. The Vinted set is 25 + Vinted fee + shipping, roughly 32-35, and then #5 and #6 would be extra singles, so the set alone already costs almost double the whole TPB.

### Recommendation
- **October:** Vol 1 TPB only (17), as planned. No singles.
- **November:** read Vol 1, then decide on the connecting set. It's a pure trophy, duplicated by the TPB, so only if you love the art. Skip #5-6 singles, since they're in Vol 1.
- **Bridge decision (also after reading Vol 1):** if hooked, #7 to #11 as standard-cover singles would be about 30-40 vs 17-20 for Vol 2 in May 2027, so about 15-20 extra to read 6 months earlier. Buy them in chunks of Vol 2 only, and don't bridge #12 onward before Vol 3 is announced. If you're lukewarm, wait for the TPBs.

## Invincible Iron Man (2022), Gerry Duggan
- The run ended at **#20** (20 issues). Ties into the X-Men "Fall of X"; Tony marries Emma Frost.
- TPBs: **Vol 1 "Demon in the Armor"** (#1-6), **Vol 2 "The Wedding of Tony Stark and Emma Frost"** (#7-12), **Vol 3 "Iron & Diamonds"** (#13-20, ISBN 9781302957094, published 2024). Your Vol 1-2 are on the way.
- Vol 3 still shows up at major booksellers (Amazon, Barnes & Noble and others). I could not confirm whether it's in print, and the low-print theory is unverified. It may be worth checking US shops and international shipping instead of paying about 20 EUR per single.
- You have #13, #19 (Skottie Young variant), #20. Missing #14-18 (five singles, about 100 EUR on eBay). Vol 3 would cover all of #13-20 in one book, so keep hunting Vol 3 first, singles as fallback. Don't buy all five at once.
- No omnibus for this run found (Wikipedia lists no Duggan Iron Man omnibus).
- **Goodreads reader ratings (2026-09-29):** Vol 1 3.75 (450 ratings), Vol 2 3.88 (378), **Vol 3 3.67 (276)**. Reviews I found call Vol 3 (#13-20) mixed, with more X-Men than Tony, though armor designs and the Emma Frost angle are praised. Some readers rank it below Cantwell's Iron Man run that came right before.

### Your situation (decided 2026-09-29)
- You have Vol 1-2 (#1-12 arriving), #13, #19 (Skottie Young variant), #20. The US import of Vol 3 is too expensive, so the missing #14-18 are the issue. You found all five on UK eBay from 3 sellers for almost 100 EUR landed (shipping and import included), about 20 each.
- **Recommendation: don't buy them yet; decide after reading Vol 1-2.** Reasons: Vol 3 is the weakest-rated volume, 100 EUR is half a monthly budget for a palate-cleanser run, and you won't reach #14 until you've read #1-13.
- If you're hooked after Vol 2 (probably Nov-Dec), buy them then, seller by seller over 2-3 months. Three sellers means three parcels and three import charges anyway, so spreading them out costs nothing extra. Ask each seller to combine shipping if they have more than one. Bookmark or watch the three listings now; the risk of waiting is that a listing sells.
- One cheap check before paying 100: search Vol 3 (ISBN 9781302957094) on Amazon Spain, Italy, France and Germany and set a Walt's back-in-stock alert. An EU copy at about 20 would beat 100 for the singles, even though you'd own #13, #19, #20 twice.
- The cost of skipping: the run ends without #14-18, which is the last third of the story. Not a good stopping point.

## Doctor Strange by Jason Aaron and Chris Bachalo (2015)
- Volume 4 ran 36 issues. **Aaron wrote #1-20.** Dennis Hopeless wrote #21-24 and John Barber #25-26. After Marvel Legacy renumbering the series continued as legacy #381-390 with **Donny Cates** (Loki as Sorcerer Supreme), then a fifth series by **Mark Waid and Jesus Saiz** (Aug 2018, 20 issues).
- **The Aaron and Bachalo Omnibus** (576 pages, Apr 2022) = Doctor Strange (2015) #1-20, Annual #1, and Doctor Strange: Last Days of Magic #1. That's Aaron's whole run, the part everybody recommends. **It does not include #21-26 or the Cates issues.**
- Aaron TPBs: *The Way of the Weird* (#1-5), *The Last Days of Magic* (#6-10), *Blood in the Aether* (#11-16), *Mr. Misery* (#17-20, Annual #1). Note that Blood in the Aether runs to #16, so the TPB split doesn't line up with the omnibus. Four TPBs plus an annual are more expensive than one omnibus at about 85 EUR, unless you find them cheap.
- Legacy numbers: #1-20 is legacy #355-374 (that's the numbering you noticed on League of Comic Geeks).

### Recommendation
- The omnibus at 84.70 on Vinted is a fair price and buys the best part of the run in one book.
- Not this month; keep it as the modern Strange palate cleanser for when you finish Epic 5 and are blocked.
- If you want to continue afterwards, Cates (legacy #381-390) is the next step, then Waid.
