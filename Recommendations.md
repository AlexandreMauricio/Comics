# Recommendations

Started **2026-11-10**. Your current top three are **Iron Man, Doctor Strange and Thor**. You read Spider-Man because he is Marvel's main hero and you grew up with him, and Daredevil because you like the lawyer angle; neither is a top-three line. Recommendations follow what you said you like: Iron Man's tech and Tony as a person, Strange's mystic atmosphere and art, Thor's mythology. Nothing here is planned or priced yet unless stated. Sources are the two reading-order sites already in the vault plus two searches on 2026-11-10; details I could not confirm are marked.

## Other characters to consider (lines, not runs)
Matching what you like: Iron Man's tech and Tony's inner life, Strange's mystic atmosphere, Thor's mythology. These would be **new, slow lines**: at the budget you already run seven lines and threads (the five lines, Venom and Avengers), so I would add at most one, and not before the Iron Man, Strange and Thor books are in hand.
1. **Fantastic Four.** Reed Richards is the closest thing to Tony's tech-genius side in the Marvel universe, and Doctor Doom (Reed's enemy, and Iron Man's and Strange's) ties it together. You already own FF Epic 1; both reading-order sites put FF Epics 1 to 3 on the route. Epic 2 was seen at 125 on Cheap-Comics, so wait for a better price.
2. **Hercules** (below). Mythology, and he already crosses into Thor.
3. **Doctor Doom, as a thread rather than a line.** He is Iron Man's rival (the *Doomquest* and Camelot stories on the Comic Book Herald page), Strange's rival (Doom becomes Sorcerer Supreme in the stories your Strange note describes) and the villain of *One World Under Doom*, which is already in the vault as a wanted omnibus.
4. **The Defenders.** Strange's own team and the Comic Book Herald treats them as part of the Strange route (Masterworks and two Epic Collections); they are the mystic side of Marvel.
5. **Silver Surfer.** The cosmic, mythic Kirby and Lee side, and a Defender; suggested by theme, I have not checked any reading order for him.
Not suggested: Captain America (you said you never cared for him) and more of the lines you dropped (Ant-Man, Hulk, Moon Knight).

## Hercules (you asked)
- **Fits Thor's mythology side.** Hercules is Marvel's Greek-myth counterpart, and he already touches your Thor books (your vault notes that two issues of the Simonson stretch sit in a Hercules omnibus).
- **Where to start:** Bob Layton's *Hercules: Prince of Power* (1982 and 1984 miniseries, 8 issues in all). Then **Greg Pak and Fred Van Lente's *Incredible Hercules*** (2008 to 2010), which readers and the Comic Book Herald treat as the main run. There is a Pak and Van Lente omnibus; the sources I found did not confirm a Hercules Epic Collection. *Herc: The Complete Series* and the Chaos War material follow.
- **Not yet checked:** prices and stock. The guides disagree on a few tie-ins, so check an issue list before buying.
- **Where it would fit:** Thor's wait or the gap after Omnibus 5. Hercules is a **new line**, so it would not displace a line book; ask me to price the omnibus when you want it.

## Thor-side mythology
- ***Journey Into Mystery* by Kieron Gillen (#622 to #645, 2011 to 2012):** the top-ranked Loki run in the guides I found (Kid Loki after Siege). Complete collections exist. It reads like a Thor-world book, and it is a common companion to Thor.
- ***Thor: The Lost Gods*:** already your Thor Epic 24.
- ***Thor: Son of Asgard*:** already in [[Side Reads]].
- *Loki: Agent of Asgard* (Al Ewing) is named as the follow-up to Gillen's run; I could not confirm anything else about it.

## Doctor Strange-side
- **Aaron and Bachalo's *Doctor Strange* (2015 to 2017):** already in the vault (omnibus 84.70 on Vinted).
- ***Doctor Strange: Into Shamballa*** (a 1980s original graphic novel, listed by the Comic Book Herald), the Defenders Masterworks and Epic Collections (the page treats "read the Defenders" as good Strange advice), and the modern Strange runs listed on both sites (2022 to 2024).

## Iron Man-side
