## Hercules (you asked)
- **Fits Thor's mythology side.** Hercules is Marvel's Greek-myth counterpart, and he already touches your Thor books (your vault notes that two issues of the Simonson stretch sit in a Hercules omnibus).
- **Where to start:** Bob Layton's *Hercules: Prince of Power* (1982 and 1984 miniseries, 8 issues in all). Then **Greg Pak and Fred Van Lente's *Incredible Hercules*** (2008 to 2010), which readers and the Comic Book Herald treat as the main run. There is a Pak and Van Lente omnibus; the sources I found did not confirm a Hercules Epic Collection. *Herc: The Complete Series* and the Chaos War material follow.
- **Not yet checked:** prices and stock. The guides disagree on a few tie-ins, so check an issue list before buying.
- **Where it would fit:** Thor's wait or the gap after Omnibus 5. Hercules is a **new line**, so it would not displace a line book; ask me to price the omnibus when you want it.

## Thor-side mythology
- ***Journey Into Mystery* by Kieron Gillen (#622 to #645, 2011 to 2012):** the top-ranked Loki run in the guides I found (Kid Loki after Siege). Complete collections exist. It reads like a Thor-world book, and it is a common companion to Thor.
- ***Thor: The Lost Gods*:** already your Thor Epic 24.
- ***Thor: Son of Asgard*:** already in [[Side Reads]].
- *Loki: Agent of Asgard* (Al Ewing) is named as the follow-up to Gillen's run; I could not confirm anything else about it.

## Doctor Strange-side
- **Aaron and Bachalo's *Doctor Strange* (2015 to 2017):** already in the vault (omnibus 84.70 on Vinted).
- ***Doctor Strange: Into Shamballa*** (a 1980s original graphic novel, listed by the Comic Book Herald), the Defenders Masterworks and Epic Collections (the page treats "read the Defenders" as good Strange advice), and the modern Strange runs listed on both sites (2022 to 2024).
- **Not priced or planned.**

## Iron Man-side
- **Extremis** (Ellis and Granov, in the February order) and **Armor Wars** (June) are already planned.
- **Matt Fraction's *Invincible Iron Man* (2008 to 2012)**, on both sites' lists; the reading-order site calls one annual in it one of the best Mandarin stories of the decade.
- **Busiek and Chen's *Iron Man* (1998 onward)**, which the Comic Book Herald says has an omnibus.
- **Cantwell's *Iron Man* (2020 to 2023)**, the run right before Duggan's.
- ***The Iron Age*** is in [[Side Reads]].
