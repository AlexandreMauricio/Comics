# Wikipedia Cross-check

Checked 2026-09-29 against the Wikipedia pages [Marvel Epic Collection](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marvel_Epic_Collection) and [Marvel Omnibus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marvel_Omnibus). Books confirmed there are marked `verified: true` with a `wikipedia_checked` date. Wikipedia can be wrong or lag behind Marvel, so keep checking prices, stock and release dates on the shops.

## What matched, what changed
- Every released Iron Man, Doctor Strange, Daredevil and Spider-Man Epic in `Epics.xlsx` matched Wikipedia: issues, page counts and editions. The sheet was clearly built from the same source.
- New data from Wikipedia: Iron Man Epic 12 (512 pages, Mar 9, 2027), Daredevil Epic 10 (520 pages, Mar 9, 2027), Spider-Man Epic 30 (480 pages, Feb 9, 2027).
- **Thor** had no Main Page data. I filled years, issue ranges, creators, pages, editions and ISBNs for all 23 released Epics.
- Also filled: Fantastic Four Epic 1-3, Avengers Epic 1 (*Earth's Mightiest Heroes*), Moon Knight Epic 1 (*Bad Moon Rising*), Ant-Man Epic 1 (*The Man in the Ant Hill*), Hulk Epic 1 (*Man or Monster?*). Those titles replace my placeholders.
- Earlier notes I corrected: Thor Omnibus 5 came out **9 Sep 2025** (I had a 2026 guess), Iron Man Omnibus 1-3 titles are *The Invincible Iron Man Vol. 1-3*, and the Armor Wars omnibus is **680 pages** on Wikipedia (retailers say 768).

### Doctor Strange
- *Master of the Mystic Arts Vol. 1* (owned, 768 pages, Mar 2025) covers Epic 3's Marvel Premiere #3-14 and Doctor Strange #1-5, and Epic 4 up to #22 plus Annual #1 and Tomb of Dracula #44. Epic 4 runs #6-28, so **#23-28 are missing** from your omnibus.
- Wikipedia lists no omnibus for the material after Epic 5 (Doctor Strange vol. 2 #52 onward) before *Sorcerer Supreme Vol. 1* starts in 1988, so the Epic 6-7 block still looks unavailable in any format.
- *Sorcerer Supreme Vol. 1* (#1-40, 1,064 pages, 2017) covers Epic 8, Epic 9 and #34-40 of Epic 10. Vol. 2 (#41-59 and more) covers the rest of Epic 10 and Epic 11.
- *Aaron & Bachalo* (Doctor Strange 2015 #1-20, Annual #1, Last Days of Magic #1, 576 pages, Apr 2022) is what you saw at 84.70 on Vinted.

### Iron Man
- Omnibus 1: Tales of Suspense #39-83 (Epic 1, most of Epic 2). Omnibus 2: #84-99, IM #1-25. Omnibus 3: IM #26-67. Omnibus 4 (26 May 2026): IM #68-112, so Epic 6 and most of Epic 7, which you own as Epics.
- Michelinie/Layton/Romita Jr Omnibus (#115-157) first came out 19 Feb 2013; the April 2027 date is a reprint.
- Armor Wars Omnibus: 13 Jan 2026. Covers Epic 13 and 9 of 20 issues of Epic 16.

### Fantastic Four (for the 125 EUR Epic 2)
- Epic 2 *The Master Plan of Doctor Doom* = FF #19-32, Annual #1-2 (448 pages).
- Omnibus 1 = FF #1-30, Annual #1. It repeats all of your Epic 1 (#1-18) and covers Epic 2's #19-30 and Annual #1, but not #31-32 or Annual #2.
- Omnibus 2 = FF #31-60, Annual #2-4. It covers the rest of Epic 2, all of Epic 3 (*The Coming of Galactus*, #33-51) and #52-60 of Epic 4.
- So Epic 2 is the cleanest fit. Omnibus 1 + 2 would cover it but repeat 18 issues you own. Price both omnibuses before deciding.
