# Comic Book Herald Check

Compared on 2026-11-08 against seven pages on comicbookherald.com (Iron Man, Doctor Strange, Daredevil, Thor, Amazing Spider-Man modern era with its "best stories before 1998" section, Venom, and the Avengers trade guide). I read each page's text and matched what it names to your Epics, omnibuses and TPBs. It sits next to [[Reading Orders Check]] (comicbookreadingorders.com).

## How the two sites differ
- **comicbookreadingorders.com** gave a short route for a first-time reader (it skips most of the early runs).
- **Comic Book Herald** gives a **fuller route that names the collected editions**, including the Epic Collections you collect. For Thor and Daredevil it lists the whole classic Epic route (Thor Epics 1 to 12 and onward, Daredevil Epics 1 to 7, then Miller). So it supports your plan to read every book in order, and it contradicts the shorter site on what is "skippable". Both views are in the vault, and the plan follows yours.

## What it confirms in your plan
- **Iron Man:** Tales of Suspense #39 to #99, the original run #1 to #115, Michelinie's #116 to #157 (the omnibus you ordered), O'Neil's #160 to #200 (Epics 10 to 12), Armor Wars (#215 to #234) and Armor Wars II (#258 to #266), the Dragon Seed Saga (#270 to #275) and #278 to #291 (the Epic 16 to 17 stretch).
- **Daredevil:** the Epics 1 to 7 as written, Battlin' Jack Murdock before them, then the Miller era, Born Again (#226 to #233), Epics 12 to 19.
- **Thor:** Epics 1 to 7, Masterworks 15 to 19 for #242 to #302, *A Kingdom Lost* (#303 to #319), *Runequest* (#320 to #336), then the Simonson run and Epics 16 onward.

- **Spider-Man:** Epics 1 to 6 and the Kraven's Last Hunt reading order (Web #31, ASM #293, Spectacular #131, Web #32, ASM #294, Spectacular #132), which is inside your Epic 17.

## Things worth knowing
- **Daredevil: The Man Without Fear #1 to #5 (1993) is inside Daredevil Epic 17** (*Into the Fire*), according to the page and your own note for that Epic. If you buy the five issues on Vinted (11.15) you will own them twice when Epic 17 arrives. That is fine if you want the origin retelling early; it is not a gap-filler.
- **Daredevil Epic 4 collects Daredevil #64 to #86 plus Iron Man #35 and #36**, so the two Iron Man issues are also in your Iron Man Epic 4 coverage.
- **Daredevil Epics 5, 6 and 7 carry crossovers:** Avengers #111, Marvel Two-in-One #3, Ghost Rider #20 and Marvel Premiere #39 to #43.
- **Between Miller and Born Again** the page puts *Daredevil: Love's Labors Lost* (#215 to #217, #219 to #222, #225 to #226). None of your books has it. The Miller omnibus you cannot find is the one that starts at #158.
- **Strange and the Defenders:** the page treats "read the Defenders" as good Strange reading order and lists the Defenders Masterworks (Vol 2 to 8) and two Defenders Epic Collections (*The Six-Fingered Hand Saga*, #92 to #109; *Ashes, Ashes*, #110 to #125). **Avengers Epic 7, *The Avengers/Defenders War*** is the crossover. None of the Defenders books is in the vault.
- **Strange Masterworks 9 to 11** are on the page too: Vol 9 is #47 to #57 (it overlaps your Epic 5), Vol 10 is #58 to #73. That agrees with skipping Vol 9.
- **Avengers, the best stories by Epic:** the page names Epic 1 (#1 to #20), *Behold the Vision* (Epic 4, #57 to #76), *Kree/Skrull War* (#88 to #97, inside Epic 5), *The Bride of Ultron* (#157 to #166, Epic 9), *The Korvac Saga* (#167 to #177, Epic 10), *Nights of Wundagore* (#181 to #187), *Heart of Stone* (#188 to #205, Epic 11), *The Legacy of Thanos* (#255 to #261, which straddles Epics 14 and the unreleased 15), *Under Siege* (Epic 16) and *Assault on Olympus* (Epic 17). That fits the Epics 2 to 17 range from the other site and ranks them: Epics 4, 5, 9, 10, 11, 16 and 17 are the strong ones.
- **Venom:** the page names the Venom Epic Collections (*Symbiosis*, *Lethal Protector*, *The Madness*, *Carnage Unleashed*, *Planet of the Symbiotes*, *The Hunger*, *Shadows of the Past*, then the Modern Era Epics *Shiver* and *Sting of the Scorpion*) and the two Venomnibus volumes. None of them is in the vault; the vault's Venom line is *Absolute Carnage*, *Venom First Host* and *Venom War*. They are a way to build a classic Venom line later if you want one.
