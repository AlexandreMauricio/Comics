# Reading Rotation

Rewritten **2026-11-10** around one idea: **all five lines move at the same pace**, in a fixed order, and the buying follows the rotation (not the other way round). It replaces the older version (in git history).

## The rule
- **Round order: Iron Man, Strange, Thor, Spider-Man, Daredevil**, then the modern books. Each line reads **one unit per round** (one Epic, or one half of an omnibus). No line reads twice before the others have read once. Iron Man, Strange and Thor are your top three, but the rotation does not give them extra turns.
- **A round is about 7 weeks.** That is what 150 a month buys: about three units a month, so about five units (one per line) every seven weeks. If you read faster than that, the lines run dry sooner and the purchases have to come earlier; tell me.
- **When a line's next book has not arrived,** its slot goes to that line's own modern books (Iron Man 2024, Duggan, Extremis; Thor *Son of Asgard*; Spider-Man *Spider-Island*; Venom) and the rotation moves on. Nothing stalls the other lines.
- **Iron Man's money goes furthest:** it already owns Epics 10, 11, 13, 14, 15 behind its gap, so it needs few purchases and still reads at the same pace.

## How the plan was checked against this
Each purchase is dated so that its book arrives before that line's turn: Daredevil Omnibus 3 in **January** (needed by round 2), Spider-Man Omnibus 4 + Epic 9 in **February** (needed by round 3), the Strange Epics 9 to 11 and Daredevil Epic 8 in **March**, Daredevil Omnibus 4 in **April**, Spider-Man Epic 10 and Thor Epic 9 in **May** (needed by round 5); Thor Epics 6 and 7 come in the **December** Cheap-Comics order and Epic 8 in **March**, since Thor Omnibus 4 is gone. The old order bought Spider-Man first and left Daredevil waiting in round 2.

## If you read faster
If you finish a unit in two or three days instead of seven weeks, each line runs dry earlier and the next unit has to arrive sooner. The budget buys about three units a month, so the rotation is paced by money, not by your reading speed.
