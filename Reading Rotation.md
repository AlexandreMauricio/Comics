## What this shows
- **Strange** is dry in October and November, then has Masterworks 10 and 11 and Epic 8 (about three units) before it waits again for Epic 9.
- **Thor** stalls from November until Omnibus 4 (planned January). That is the visible price of putting the Michelinie omnibus and the Strange volumes first.
- **Daredevil** stalls after Omnibus 2 until Omnibus 3 (105, discount may end) or Epic 9's gap is filled.
- **Spider-Man** has the deepest stock (Epics 3, 4, Omnibus 3, Epic 7 is five units), then hits the Epic 8 gap.
- **Iron Man** has only Epic 8 until April, then seven books in a row. The modern runs fill the five-month gap well.
- **Fill books for stalled lines** are cheap per issue: Daredevil Vol 1 (2026, 13.50), Thor Gorr (12.79, but it releases Mar 9), *Journey Into Mystery* (24.99), the Iron Man 2022 and 2026 TPBs.

## Supply against pace
- At 5 days per unit you read about 6 units a month. At about 150 per month you buy roughly 3 units. Unless the pace slows to about one unit per 10 days, you run out during the winter.
- The stock of ready units is about 14 now. At 6 a month that is gone by December, with only the new arrivals above refilling it.

## How to read this note
Treat it as a map of where each line stands, not a promise. After each purchase or arrival, I refresh the readiness.
